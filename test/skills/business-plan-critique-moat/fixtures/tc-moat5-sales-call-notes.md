# Minutely — Series A Memo

## What Minutely does

Minutely joins a sales team's video calls, produces a structured summary within
a minute of the call ending, and writes the next steps, objections and deal
stage straight into the team's CRM. Reps stop typing notes; managers see every
deal's status without chasing anyone. We sell to sales teams of 10 to 200 reps
at $45 per rep per month.

## Traction

- 2,300 paying teams and 31,000 paid seats.
- Annualised revenue of $16.7M, up from $4.1M a year ago.
- 94% of calls we record happen on Convene, the video platform that most of our
  customers standardised on during the shift to remote selling. The remainder
  are split across two smaller platforms.
- Net revenue retention of 131%, as teams expand seats after a pilot.

## Why teams choose us

Our summaries are better than generic transcription tools because we are
trained on sales conversations. Minutely recognises pricing objections,
competitor mentions, procurement steps and verbal commitments, and it maps them
to the fields in the customer's CRM. Our accuracy on next-step extraction,
measured against rep-edited notes, is 91%.

## Moat

Our advantage has three layers.

**Sales-specific models.** We have processed more than nine million minutes of
sales conversations. Each rep edit to a summary is a labelled correction, and we
retrain monthly. Our extraction accuracy has risen from 78% to 91% in eighteen
months.

**CRM depth.** We write to custom fields, trigger CRM workflows and respect each
customer's deal-stage definitions. Setting this mapping up takes a sales-ops
lead most of a week, and it becomes part of how the team runs its pipeline
review. Teams that have completed CRM mapping churn at under 1% a month.

**Manager workflows.** Forecast calls and coaching reviews are built on Minutely
summaries. Replacing us would mean rebuilding the weekly operating rhythm of the
sales floor.

## Distribution

Most new teams start when a rep installs Minutely from the Convene app directory
and invites the bot into calls; prospects see "Minutely is taking notes" and a
share of them sign up for their own teams. When five or more reps at a company
are active, our sales team reaches out to the head of sales to convert the group
into a team plan. Around 60% of new revenue starts this way; the rest comes from
outbound to sales leaders and from CRM partner referrals.

## Competition and why incumbents won't catch us

The obvious question is whether Convene will build this itself. We do not
think so. Convene is a video company. Its roadmap is about call quality, webinars
and its phone product, and its leadership has repeatedly said that it wants a
healthy ecosystem of apps in its directory. It is also a large public company
with thousands of employees, and large companies are slow; by the time Convene
decided to build sales-specific notes, we would be years ahead in model quality
and CRM depth. The same logic applies to the major CRM vendors, which are busy
with their own platform migrations and have historically partnered for call
recording rather than building it.

Standalone call-recording companies are our direct competitors. Two are larger
than us, but both started as recording and coaching tools for large
enterprises and are expensive and slow to deploy. We win on price and
time-to-value for mid-sized teams.

We therefore do not see a credible near-term threat from incumbents, and our
plan does not budget for one. Our pricing assumes we hold $45 per seat for the
next three years.
