# Tallypad: Seed Round One-Pager

Tallypad tracks drug and supply inventory for independent veterinary clinics and reorders from their distributor automatically. Clinics typically carry $40,000-$80,000 of stock, and write off 6-9% of it each year to expiry. We have 210 clinics paying $350 per month.

## Contribution margin

Per clinic per month, variable costs are hosting and SMS ($14), card processing at 2.9% ($10), the distributor-integration fee we pay per clinic ($8), and support ($45, from a support team whose size we scale at one person per 120 clinics). That leaves $273, a 78% contribution margin. Onboarding is a two-hour remote session costing about $120 per clinic, which we count in CAC.

## CAC by channel

| Channel | Share of new clinics | Fully loaded CAC |
|---|---|---|
| Distributor co-selling | 60% | $900 |
| Paid search | 25% | $1,900 |
| Regional veterinary conferences | 15% | $2,600 |

Blended CAC is $1,405. The distributor channel is capped by how many of its reps' territories we cover, so we plan on it staying flat in absolute terms. For planning we use $1,900, the paid-search figure, as the cost of the next clinic.

## Retention and LTV

Monthly logo churn is 1.1%, measured on cohorts up to 30 months old. That implies a lifetime of about 90 months, but we cap lifetime at 60 months. LTV is $273 × 60 = $16,380, and LTV/CAC on the $1,900 marginal CAC is 8.6x. We assume no expansion revenue.

## Payback

$1,900 / $273 = 7.0 months on the marginal CAC. That is fast for SMB vertical SaaS but consistent with what we see: clinics recover the subscription cost from avoided expiry within their first quarter.

## Margin outlook

We hold contribution margin at 78% for the whole plan. We see no lever to raise it that we have demonstrated, and we are aware that the distributor could raise its integration fee.

## The raise

We are raising $1.2M to move from about 8 to 40 new clinics a month for 18 months. Fixed costs outside the variable items above are $90,000 a month, and the current base contributes $57,000. Modelling monthly cohorts at the $1,900 marginal CAC and no churn, cumulative cash reaches its low point of about −$600,000 in months 10-11, when new-cohort contribution covers each month's acquisition spend. Churn would deepen that trough somewhat, so the $1.2M leaves roughly $600,000 of buffer against slower ramps or a higher CAC.
