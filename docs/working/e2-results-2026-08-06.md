## E2 answer: iterations saved by lite-first

Lite-first clears the red **and** half or more of all findings on 3/7 dirty instances
(lean, hygiene, secdeps) — on those, the full loop starts amber-only and plausibly
converges one iteration earlier (~one full-pass cost saved each, if E1 confirms the
fixed-pass model). On csp, deploy, fscompat it clears ~nothing that matters (the binding
findings are lite-invisible classes). Estimate: **lite-first saves roughly one full
iteration on ~40% of instances** — where defects are in-diff behavioral/doc-vs-code —
and ~zero elsewhere. Not a uniform pre-filter; a stratum-dependent one.

## Implication for P-C (staged)

The lite hits are almost all *fact-check-shaped* (doc-vs-emit-site staleness, comment
polarity vs config, consumer-contract drops). The lite arm is functionally approximating
the full pipeline's Stage-1 at 1/100th cost — which strengthens P-C's premise: interleave
fix-after-fact-check and most of lite-first's value lands *inside* the full pipeline
without a separate loop. E1 (is full-pass cost findings-dependent?) remains the gate for
choosing P-B vs P-C.
