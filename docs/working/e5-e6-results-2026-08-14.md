## What E5/E6 establish

1. **Coverage profiles, not recall points, are the story.** E5 ≈ E4 in aggregate (46% vs
   49–54%) but the sets differ: E5 uniquely lands lean-R1 and hyg-A1 (cross-file classes
   no prior arm touched) and firms up D3/D5; E4 uniquely lands csp-C4 and more of
   postfix. E6 uniquely lands csp-R1. Every new process keeps finding things every other
   process missed — the union argument strengthens with each arm.
2. **Agentic repo access buys exactly the predicted classes.** The cross-file misses
   E2/E4 could never reach (hyg-A1, lean-R1) fall to one cheap agentic pass. What still
   resists *every* arm: repo-wide enumeration/convention (cor-A1/A2, fsc-A2),
   test-strategy (csp-A4, fsc-A4), structural type-seam reasoning (pf-R1), and D6.
3. **False attestation (H5) appears in the built-in skill too**: E5-csp certified
   R1/R2/A2 as checked-and-clean; E5-postfix endorsed pf-A1's fail-open default; E6-csp
   reasoned itself out of A1. "Confirmed clean" prose from any single process remains
   untrustworthy.
4. **E5 is a discovery engine off-ledger**: 6 verified TRUE-substantive findings beyond
   the 43 known issues (candidate ledger rows, stratum E) at zero confirmed FPs — its
   in-container *execution* of lint/CI (secdeps) is something no other arm did.
5. **Ultra's opacity is a real eval cost**: refuted findings unrecoverable, no per-run
   cost surface, one verified finding silently dropped at synthesis. For eval purposes
   E5-class arms are auditable; ultra is scoreable only on its kept set. Confirmed
   2026-08-14 via docs check: there is **no documented access path** to a crashed run's
   session logs or partial artifacts (no --resume/--logs/artifact retrieval; docs state
   stopped reviews "don't return partial findings"; the "see session logs" error message
   points at nothing user-reachable) — so secdeps' 8 raw findings are permanently
   unrecoverable short of contacting support with the session URL.
