Compare two managed search services for our product catalogue.

**Items:** Lumen Search, Harrow Index
**Criteria:** monthly cost, p95 query latency, relevance tuning effort

Facts from a one-week trial on the same 2.1 million products and the same replayed query log:

- **Lumen Search**: $3,800/month. p95 latency 48 ms. Relevance was good out of the box; our merchandiser needed about half a day to tune synonyms.
- **Harrow Index**: $1,450/month. p95 latency 165 ms. Relevance needed about four days of tuning (custom analysers, boosting rules) to match Lumen.

We haven't decided yet whether cost or speed matters more to us this year.
