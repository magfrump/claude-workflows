### Where the archetypes conflict

  front, and screened them favourably ("Limitations are stated before the results"). The enthusiast stops there.
  By the profile's rule, the declared ILIAD reader decides, and the other need moves to a layer. Proposed: keep a

  the maintainer better anyway.
- **Lean names in prose** (F2 against the builder). The builder used them as an index. The others found them
  opaque. Proposed: prose says what each object is, and the name rides in the bracket.

## Notes for the `reader-testing` skill

- **It doubles as a referee pass.** The maintainer and builder sessions found real correctness issues (A1–A6).
  The skill needs a separate lane for them: verify each one, don't fold it into exposition, and block posting on
  it.
- **The knowledge limit should point to documents, not a list.** The conjecturer's hand-written notation list
  left out PA_bin and Bud-terms, and the reader marked them unknown.
- **The blind cut should end where the opening ends.** Lines 1–11 ended on the densest paragraph. That measured
  how dense the caveats are, which is itself a finding (F14), but it wants a deliberate choice.
- **Ask for answers before and after opening the linked source** (the enthusiast's suggestion). Whether the post
  stands alone is the difference.
- **Ask readers to flag reconstructions** (answers assembled from several fragments), as the enthusiast did
  unprompted. Assembly is the "buried" signal.
- **Generalize the label search** to any bracket (`\[[A-Za-z]`). The suggested one missed "[modelling
  assumption]".
- **Tell the maintainer how far to read the attachment:** statements yes, proofs no. And ask for wall-clock
  times: that is how the build-time mismatch was found.
- **Isolation is advisory.** The builder wrote one file outside its folder. Run readers in their own scratch
  folders and check afterwards.
- **Cost:** about 530k subagent tokens across five sessions. Builder 158k, maintainer 127k, pilot 93k, enthusiast
  86k, blind take 61k; the builder took about 6 minutes, mostly the Mathlib fetch. Convergence counts were the
  most useful output, because three or four readers hitting the same sentence is hard to argue with.
