# Ledgerly: Series A Investment Memo

## What Ledgerly is

Ledgerly does monthly bookkeeping for small businesses, mostly service firms with $500K-$5M in revenue: agencies, clinics, trades contractors. A client connects its bank accounts and card feeds, and each month we deliver reconciled books, a profit-and-loss statement, and a short commentary from their assigned bookkeeper. Clients pay a flat $450 per month.

What makes Ledgerly different from a traditional bookkeeping firm is our categorization model, which suggests the account for each transaction so bookkeepers review and approve instead of keying entries by hand.

We have 2,300 clients and are raising a $15M Series A.

## Unit economics today

| Per client, per month | Amount |
|---|---|
| Price | $450 |
| Bookkeeper labour | $230 |
| Software, bank-feed fees, hosting, processing | $35 |
| **Gross profit** | **$185 (41%)** |

Bookkeeper labour is our largest cost. Each bookkeeper handles about 28 clients. Along with categorization, the work includes reconciling accounts, chasing clients for missing receipts, resolving payroll and loan entries, and writing the monthly commentary, which clients tell us is the part they value most.

**CAC.** Fully loaded CAC is $1,800. About half of new clients come through our partner program with CPA firms, who refer clients that are too small for them, at a CAC of $1,200. The other half come through paid search at $2,400. Both have been stable for a year.

**Retention.** Monthly churn is 2%, measured across 30 months of cohorts, which implies an average client lifetime of 50 months. Churn is mostly business closures and clients outgrowing us into a full-time controller.

**At today's margin:** LTV is $185 / 2% = $9,250, LTV/CAC is 5.1x, and CAC payback is about 10 months.

## Where margins go

Our model, and the valuation we are proposing, is built on steady-state gross margin of 75%, which we expect to reach within 24 months.

At 75%, gross profit is $337.50 per client per month:

- **LTV:** $337.50 / 2% = $16,875
- **LTV/CAC:** 9.4x
- **CAC payback:** 5.3 months

The path to 75% comes from automation. Today our model categorizes 22% of transactions with enough confidence that bookkeepers approve them without editing. We expect this to reach 80% within 18 months as the model trains on our growing transaction history. At that level, we estimate bookkeeper labour falls from $230 to about $78 per client per month, and each bookkeeper can handle roughly three times as many clients.

Over the past twelve months the auto-approval rate rose from 18% to 22%. The next version of the model, which our two machine-learning engineers are building now, uses a larger foundation model and each client's own history, and we believe it will produce a step change.

## Comparable companies

Pure-software accounting tools report gross margins of 75-85%. We see Ledgerly as a software company that currently delivers part of its service through people, and we think it should be valued on its steady-state software margin rather than today's services-like margin.

## Use of funds

- $7M sales and marketing: grow the CPA partner program and scale paid search
- $5M engineering and machine learning: grow the ML team from two to eight
- $3M operations and buffer

We plan to reach 6,000 clients in 24 months. Our hiring plan assumes bookkeeper headcount grows from 82 to 110 over that period, far slower than client growth, because of the automation gains above.

## Why we will win

Small businesses are poorly served. Traditional firms are expensive and slow, and do-it-yourself software leaves owners doing their own books at 11pm. Ledgerly offers a human bookkeeper at close to software prices, and every client we add makes the categorization model better. As automation compounds, our cost to serve falls while our prices hold, and that is the core of the investment case.
