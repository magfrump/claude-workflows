# Launch plan: Self-serve returns for Oakbarrow Furniture

**Owner:** Customer Experience product team · **Launch:** 1 September

## Problem

Customers who want to return an item today have to phone or email Customer
Service. Average time to get a return started is 2.5 days, and "how do I return
this?" is our second most common contact reason. Returns are a known reason
first-time customers do not order again: in last year's survey, 22% of
customers who did not reorder mentioned the returns process.

## What we are launching

A "Return an item" button on each order in the customer's account. The customer
picks the items and a reason, uploads a photo for damaged goods, and chooses a
collection date. They get an instant confirmation email saying their return has
been requested and a refund will follow once it is approved.

Behind the button, nothing changes in how refunds are issued. Each request lands
in the Returns queue in our ERP, where the Refunds desk in Finance Operations
checks the order, the photo and the reason, books the courier collection, and
approves the refund. The desk is three people. Each request takes about eight
minutes, and between them they get through around 45 a day alongside their
other reconciliation work. Today we receive about 35 return requests a day by
phone and email, and the desk clears them within two working days.

## Expected impact

Based on the pilot with our trade customers and on benchmarks from the
furniture retailers in our peer group, we expect return requests to roughly
triple once returns are self-serve, because customers who today give up on a
return (or never start one) will now complete it. We consider this a good
outcome: a customer who returns easily is more likely to order again, and the
lifetime value model in the business case assumes a 6-point lift in repeat
purchase rate over twelve months.

## Rollout

- **1 September:** launch to all customers, announced in the order-confirmation
  email and on the Help pages.
- **Week 1:** product team monitors button usage, completion rate, and errors.
- **Week 4:** review repeat-purchase leading indicators.

## Metrics

- Share of returns started online (target: 80% by end of October).
- Contacts to Customer Service about returns (target: down 60%).
- Customer satisfaction with the returns process (target: 4.3 out of 5).

## Dependencies

- ERP integration for the Returns queue: built and tested.
- Courier booking API: already used for deliveries.
- Help-centre content: drafted.

## Decision requested

Approve the 1 September launch to all customers.
