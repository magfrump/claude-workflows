Artifact Types

Select the output types appropriate for your text and use case.

  • Deductive (Lean): Structured deductive argument with mathematical notation, logical steps, and a machine-verifiable Lean 4 proof.
  • Causal Graph: Directed graph of variables, causal relationships, confounders, and mechanisms.
  • Statistical Model: Variables with roles, testable hypotheses with null hypotheses, and suggested statistical tests.
  • Property Tests: Invariants, preconditions, postconditions, and data generators as executable test specs.
  • Dialectical Map: Map of distinct viewpoints, tensions between them, and a proposed synthesis.
  • Counterexamples: Adversarial analysis identifying specific scenarios that could falsify the claim, with plausibility ratings.
