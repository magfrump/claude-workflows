# ChairTime: Seed Round Memo

## What we do

ChairTime is scheduling and patient-recall software for independent dental practices. A practice plugs ChairTime into its existing practice-management system, and we fill cancelled chair time by texting patients who are overdue for a cleaning or who asked to be moved earlier. Independent practices lose an estimated 8-12% of hygienist hours to late cancellations and no-shows, and most still work the phone by hand to fill those gaps.

The company was founded 20 months ago by Dr. Priya Patel, who ran a two-chair practice in Columbus for nine years, and Marcus Oyelaran, previously an engineering lead at a healthcare payments company.

## Traction

- 140 paying practices, all on the single $300/month plan
- 1.5% monthly logo churn, measured across every cohort since launch (the oldest is 20 months old)
- Gross margin of 80% after hosting, SMS delivery, the practice-management integration fees we pay per location, and card processing
- Practices using ChairTime recover a median of 11 hygienist hours per month

## Customer acquisition

Over the last 20 months we have spent $210,000 on acquisition in total, fully loaded. That includes paid search, booths at two regional dental trade shows, a share of one account executive's salary, and a $150 account credit we give to any practice that refers another. Dividing by 140 practices gives a CAC of $1,500.

Our early growth has been driven mostly by word of mouth. Dr. Patel is active in two Ohio study clubs and her dental-school alumni group, and 100 of our 140 practices came to us through those relationships, typically after a colleague demonstrated the product at a study-club meeting. Those practices cost us only the $150 referral credit. The remaining 40 practices came through paid search and the trade shows, which account for the rest of the spend.

## Unit economics

At $300/month and 80% gross margin, each practice contributes $240/month. With 1.5% monthly churn, the expected customer lifetime is about 67 months, giving an LTV of $16,000.

- **LTV/CAC:** $16,000 / $1,500 = 10.7x
- **CAC payback:** $1,500 / $240 = 6.25 months

These are strong numbers for vertical SaaS, and we think they reflect a product that sells itself once a dentist sees it working in a peer's office.

Support is handled by a two-person team that also runs onboarding, which is a one-hour remote session. We include the cost of that team in the gross-margin figure above, since its workload grows with practice count. We have not raised prices since launch and do not assume a price increase in the model.

## Gross margin outlook

We do not project margin expansion. SMS and integration fees scale linearly with practices, and we expect gross margin to stay in the 78-81% range. The one risk we watch is our largest practice-management partner raising its per-location integration fee, which would cost us about two points of margin.

## The plan

Most of Dr. Patel's study-club and alumni network is now either a customer or has declined. Over the next 24 months we plan to add 900 practices, and we expect the large majority of that growth to come from paid search, the national trade-show circuit, and a two-person outbound team calling practices in Ohio, Indiana, Michigan and Pennsylvania. We estimate roughly 80% of new practices will come from these paid channels.

Our model holds CAC at $1,500, so acquiring 900 practices requires a $1.35M acquisition budget. With a 6-month payback, cohorts start funding the next quarter's acquisition quickly, and the model reaches cash-flow breakeven in month 19 at roughly 1,000 practices.

## The ask

We are raising a $2.5M seed round:

- $1.35M acquisition (paid search, trade shows, outbound team)
- $0.75M product and engineering (two additional integrations)
- $0.40M general and administrative, plus buffer

We believe ChairTime can become the default recall tool for the roughly 120,000 independent dental practices in the US, and that the unit economics above give us an unusually capital-efficient path to get there.
