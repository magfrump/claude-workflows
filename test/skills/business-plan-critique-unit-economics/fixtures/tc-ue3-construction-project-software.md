# Groundwork: Growth Plan and Series A Financial Narrative

## The business

Groundwork is project-controls software for mid-sized commercial general contractors, firms doing $50M-$400M a year in work. It pulls schedules, change orders, subcontractor invoices and daily field reports into one place, so a project executive can see margin fade on a job weeks before the monthly cost report would show it. We sell annual contracts at an average of $18,000 per customer per year ($1,500 per month), priced per active project.

We have 150 customers today, up from 90 a year ago.

## Gross margin

Gross margin is 82%. Cost of revenue includes cloud hosting, the construction cost-index data we license per customer, and card and ACH processing. It has been stable within a point for six quarters.

## Customer success and implementation

Contractors are not quick adopters, and we have learned that a customer who is not live on at least three projects within 60 days usually churns at renewal. Two teams make sure that doesn't happen.

- **Implementation.** Four implementation specialists, fully loaded at $140,000 each ($560,000 a year). Each new customer gets a three-week implementation: mapping their cost codes, connecting their accounting system, and training project managers on site. Last year the team implemented the 60 customers we signed.
- **Customer success.** Six customer success managers, fully loaded at $110,000 each ($660,000 a year). Each CSM owns about 25 accounts, holds monthly job-review calls with the project executives, and runs the renewal.

We treat both teams as fixed operating overhead, reported below gross margin alongside engineering and G&A, and the unit economics below do not include them.

## Retention

Annual logo churn is 10%. Net revenue retention is 104%, driven by customers adding active projects as they get comfortable with the product. Our oldest cohort is now three years old, and churn has been flat across cohorts.

## Acquisition

Last year we signed 60 new customers for $900,000 in fully loaded sales and marketing spend (salaries, commission, events, and programs):

| Channel | New customers | CAC |
|---|---|---|
| Outbound (AE-led) | 36 | $17,500 |
| Inbound (content, webinars, referrals) | 24 | $11,250 |
| **Blended** | **60** | **$15,000** |

Outbound CAC has risen about 10% year over year as we have moved beyond the contractors we knew personally. We model outbound CAC rising another 10% a year.

## Unit economics

- **Monthly gross profit per customer:** $1,500 × 82% = $1,230
- **LTV:** $14,760 annual gross profit / 10% annual churn = $147,600
- **LTV/CAC:** 9.8x
- **CAC payback:** $15,000 / $1,230 = 12.2 months

A 12-month payback and a near-10x LTV/CAC put Groundwork in the top quartile of vertical SaaS.

## The three-year plan

We plan to reach 600 customers in three years. The key point in the model is operating leverage: because implementation and customer success are fixed overhead, quadrupling the customer base does not require growing those teams. Today they cost $1.22M a year against $2.7M of revenue, about 45%. At 600 customers and $10.8M of revenue, the same $1.22M falls to about 11% of revenue, which is most of the path from today's operating loss to a 25% EBITDA margin in year three.

Engineering grows from 14 to 22 people over the plan, mainly for an owner-facing reporting module and an integration with the two largest construction ERPs. G&A grows modestly.

## Gross-margin outlook

We project gross margin staying at 81-83%. Hosting costs scale with projects, not customers, and our data-license contract is priced per customer with a volume discount that starts at 400 customers, worth about one point of margin. We have not included the discount in the base case.

## The ask

We are raising a $14M Series A:

- $7M sales and marketing, to grow the outbound team from four to ten AEs
- $5M engineering
- $2M G&A and buffer

We believe Groundwork's economics, a 12-month payback today and operating leverage that turns growth into margin, make it one of the most capital-efficient ways to build a category leader in construction software.
