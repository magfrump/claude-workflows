# Sunday Afternoon Hours at the Eastgate Branch: A 12-Week Pilot

**To:** Library Board, Fernhollow Public Library
**From:** Branch Services Manager
**Decision requested:** Approve a 12-week pilot of Sunday opening at one branch

## What we propose

Open the Eastgate branch from 1 to 5 p.m. on Sundays for twelve weeks, starting the first Sunday in February, and decide at the April board meeting whether to continue, change, or stop.

## Why Eastgate

Eastgate is the only branch within walking distance of the two largest apartment complexes on the east side, and its weekday afternoon visits are the highest in the system. In last spring's patron survey, 212 of the 640 Eastgate respondents (33 percent) said weekend hours were the change they most wanted, more than at any other branch. Saturday visits at Eastgate already run 40 percent above its weekday average, which suggests unmet weekend demand rather than a demand we would have to create.

## Staffing and cost

Sunday shifts will be covered by four staff who volunteered for them, at the premium rate in our current staff agreement. The cost is $310 per Sunday for staff and about $45 for heating and lighting, so roughly $4,260 for the full pilot. It comes from the branch's unspent programming line, which had $11,800 left at the end of the last quarter. No other branch's hours change, and no weekday hours are cut.

## What we will measure

- Door-counter visits each Sunday, compared with Saturday visits in the same weeks;
- Checkouts and computer sessions on Sundays;
- How many Sunday visitors hold cards registered to addresses more than a mile from any branch;
- A one-page exit survey, offered at the desk, asking whether the visitor could have come at another time.

We will treat the pilot as worth continuing if Sunday visits average at least 60 percent of Saturday visits by the final four weeks and at least a quarter of surveyed visitors say they could not have come at another time. Below either line, we will recommend stopping or trying a different branch.

## Risks

Early Sundays may be quiet while word gets around, so the decision rests on the last four weeks, not the first. Staff who volunteered may want to stop; two further staff have said they would cover if needed. If the pilot ends, patrons will have had notice from the start that it is a twelve-week trial, and the posted signage will say so.

## Recommendation

Approve the 12-week Sunday pilot at Eastgate, funded from the branch programming line, with a report and recommendation at the April board meeting.
