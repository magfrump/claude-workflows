# Claimwell: Market Case for a £600k Seed Round

Claimwell submits and tracks private-insurance claims for independent
physiotherapy clinics in the UK. It plugs into a clinic's existing booking
system and turns each completed appointment into an electronic claim. We have 23
paying clinics.

## Market size

There are about 6,500 private physiotherapy clinics in the UK, counting
independents and small chains of up to five sites. At £300 a month (£3,600 a
year), that is a total market of £23.4 million. We checked it against the
spend we can see: our pilot clinics were already paying an average of £380 a
month to billing bureaus or part-time admin staff for the same work.

We count as serviceable only clinics that take insured patients and use one of
the four booking systems we integrate with. From trade-association survey data,
about 55% of clinics meet both conditions: 3,575 clinics, or £12.9 million a
year. This is a small market, and we are raising a small round to match.

## Why now

In April 2025, Albion Health and Crestmoor, which together cover most privately
insured physiotherapy patients, stopped accepting emailed and paper invoices
and now require claims through their provider APIs. Clinics that billed by email
must now integrate or pay a bureau. Two thirds of our current customers signed
up within three months of the switch.

## Share

Our beachhead is Greater Manchester and Yorkshire, where we have 19 of our 23
customers and about 120 serviceable clinics we can visit in person. We aim for
30% of them (36 clinics) by the end of year two, sold by the two founders. By
the end of year five we project 160 clinics nationally, 4.5% of the serviceable
market and £576,000 in ARR. That is a profitable business at our cost base, not
a venture-scale one, and we are not presenting it as one.

## Comparable companies

Dentaclaim did the same for UK dental practices when insurers moved to
electronic claims in 2019, and reached about 400 practices in five years.
Optiform tried the same model for opticians but found that few optician
patients claim on insurance, and closed after three years. Physiotherapy looks
closer to dental: insured patients are a large share of visits and claims
recur weekly during a course of treatment.

## Risks to the market case

If a booking system builds its own claims module, clinics on that system leave
our serviceable market; we would lose about a quarter of it if the largest one
did. We watch the four vendors' roadmaps closely.
