# Decision brief: webhook payload format for the Ledgerline partner API

**Decision.** We are choosing the payload format for the outbound webhook events
(`invoice.paid`, `invoice.overdue`, `payout.settled`) that the Ledgerline
partner API will start sending on 1 March. The three options are a flat JSON
object, a nested JSON envelope with a `data` block, or CloudEvents-style JSON.

**Who is deciding.** The integrations team (five engineers) owns the partner API
and will make the call at Thursday's design review. Product has delegated it to
us entirely.

**Context.** 312 accounting and payroll vendors are signed up for the partner
programme. At launch every one of them will write their own parser against
whatever we ship; our partner SDK does not cover webhooks until next year. The
partner agreement that went out in January commits Ledgerline to keep every
published event field and its meaning unchanged for 24 months after launch.
None of the three candidate formats carries a schema version field, and we
have not planned one.

**How we are framing it.** We see this as a low-stakes formatting choice. The
events are generated by one serializer class, so switching formats is roughly
a day of work on our side, and we can always revisit it after launch once we
see how partners use the events. For that reason we want to decide in one
meeting, with no written comparison, and keep the design review to thirty
minutes.

**Criteria we plan to use.**

1. Payload size (all three are under 2 KB for typical events).
2. How pleasant the payload is to read in our own logs.
3. Serializer effort on our side (flat is simplest, CloudEvents needs a small
   adapter).

**Constraints.** Launch date is fixed by the partner agreement. The
integrations team has capacity for about two weeks of webhook work before
launch.
