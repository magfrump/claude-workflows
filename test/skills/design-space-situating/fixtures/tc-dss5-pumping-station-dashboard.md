# Decision brief: alarm dashboard for Harrowgate Water's pumping stations

**Decision.** Harrowgate Water is choosing a new dashboard for its 64 sewage
and clean-water pumping stations. The options are (a) a single network health
score from 0 to 100 with a red/amber/green status per region, (b) a regional
map with one status tile per station, or (c) a station-level view listing each
pump's pressure, flow and alarm history.

**Who is deciding.** The digital transformation steering group: the Chief
Operating Officer, the finance director, the head of regulation and two
programme managers. The group scores each option in a review meeting and the
highest total wins.

**Context.** The dashboard will be the main screen in the control room, which
is staffed around the clock by two operators per shift. When an alarm fires at
3am the night-shift operator decides whether to dispatch a crew, restart a
pump remotely, or wait. Operators say most real incidents start as a slow
pressure drift on a single pump over two or three hours, and that they judge
urgency by comparing a pump against its own trend and its neighbours. The
same dashboard will also feed a monthly slide in the board pack and the
regulator's quarterly performance return.

**Scoring criteria agreed by the steering group.**

1. Can a board member understand network status in under ten seconds?
2. Does it map directly onto the regulator's quarterly return?
3. Does it look modern in stakeholder demonstrations?
4. Licence and build cost.

On these criteria option (a) scores 17 of 20, (b) 13 and (c) 8.

**Constraints.** Contract award is in six weeks. The old dashboard's licence
ends in five months.
