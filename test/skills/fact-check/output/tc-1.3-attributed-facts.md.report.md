## Verdict for C1: "Minnesota legalized recreational cannabis in 2023"

**Verdict:** Accurate
**Confidence:** Medium
**Provenance:** observed
**Scrutiny:** abstract

H.F. 100 was enacted as Minnesota Session Laws 2023, chapter 63. The Revisor of Statutes describes it as legalizing and limiting adult-use cannabis and creating the Office of Cannabis Management. Contemporary reporting (AP via Washington Times, CBS Minnesota, UPI) agrees it became law in 2023. Two independent primary sources exist (the Revisor's session law and the governor's press release), but I only saw both as snippets, so this is capped at Medium. Reading the chapter 63 text in full would lift it to High.

**Sources:** MN Revisor of Statutes, Laws 2023 ch. 63 (primary); MN Governor's Office press release, 2023 (primary); AP / Washington Times, 2023-05-30; CBS Minnesota, 2023
**Citation:** https://www.revisor.mn.gov/laws/2023/0/Session+Law/Chapter/63/

## Verdict for C4: "took effect on August 1st"

**Verdict:** Mostly accurate
**Confidence:** Medium
**Provenance:** observed
**Scrutiny:** abstract

August 1, 2023 is the date most sources give for legal adult possession and home growing. The Minnesota House New Laws summary says that from Aug. 1, 2023, personal possession within the legal limits is no longer a crime. Stinson LLP and Fortune say the same. However, the law did not take effect all at once:

- The House Research summary says most provisions took effect July 1, 2023, unless a section names a different date.
- Some provisions took effect June 30 or May 31, 2023 (taxes and limits on local taxes).
- Licensed retail sales were delayed until 2025.
- The MSBA *Bench & Bar* article says the bill's adult possession and use section itself is dated July 1, 2023, with the criminal-law changes delayed to August 1.

So "August 1" is right for when personal-use legalization took effect in practice. Saying "the legislation" as a whole took effect that day is imprecise. I could not open the MSBA article in full, so the July 1 detail is snippet-level only.

**Sources:** MN House New Laws, 2023 (primary); MN House Research bill summary H.F. 100 (primary); MN Revenue legislative bulletin, 2023-06; Stinson LLP, 2023; MSBA *Bench & Bar*, 2023-09-01; Fortune, 2023-08-02
**Citation:** https://www.house.mn.gov/NewLaws/story/2023/5516 ("beginning Aug. 1, 2023, personal possession… is no longer a crime", as it appeared in the search snippet)

## Claims Requiring Author Attention

- **C4**: August 1, 2023 is right for legal personal possession and home growing. Other parts of the law took effect on other dates (May 31, June 30 and July 1, 2023), and retail sales began in 2025. The draft also does not give the year with "August 1st."
