# Kibble & Co. Series A Narrative

## Summary

Kibble & Co. delivers fresh, portioned dog food on a subscription. Owners fill in a profile for their dog, we recommend a recipe and portion size, and meals arrive frozen every four weeks. We have 11,400 active subscribers across the Northeast and Mid-Atlantic, growing 9% month over month for the last two quarters, and we are raising a $12M Series A to scale paid acquisition and open a second co-packing partner in the Southeast.

## Product and pricing

Subscribers pay per box, and the average order is $62 per month. Recipes are cooked by a contract co-packer in New Jersey to our specification and shipped in insulated boxes with dry ice. We run no retail and no wholesale. Every subscriber is direct.

## Gross margin

Our gross margin is 38%. It covers ingredients, co-packing fees, packaging, cold-chain shipping, and payment processing. Fresh food is a lower-margin business than kibble, and we have priced for that rather than chasing a premium price point. Each subscriber contributes $23.56 per month after these costs.

We do not project gross-margin expansion in this raise. The second co-packer is about delivery times and redundancy, not cost. Ingredient prices have been flat for four quarters, and our contract with the New Jersey co-packer locks the per-pound fee through next year.

## Retention

Monthly subscriber churn is 6%, and it has held between 5.7% and 6.3% for every monthly cohort since launch 14 months ago. Most churn happens in the first two boxes. Subscribers who reach box four churn at under 3% a month. We have not assumed any improvement in the model.

## Acquisition

Last quarter we added 3,600 subscribers at a fully loaded CAC of $180, including creative production, agency fees, and the free first box:

| Channel | New subscribers | CAC |
|---|---|---|
| Paid social | 2,100 | $210 |
| Influencer partnerships | 900 | $160 |
| Referral (give $20, get $20) | 600 | $105 |
| **Blended** | **3,600** | **$180** |

Paid-social CAC has stayed between $200 and $215 for four straight quarters even as we tripled spend, which suggests we are nowhere near saturating our audiences.

## Unit economics

- **Average subscriber lifetime:** 1 / 6% = 16.7 months
- **LTV:** $62 average order / 6% monthly churn = $1,033
- **LTV/CAC:** $1,033 / $180 = 5.7x
- **CAC payback:** $180 / $23.56 contribution per month = 7.6 months

A 5.7x LTV/CAC ratio is well above the 3x threshold investors typically look for in subscription consumer businesses. It tells us that every dollar we put into paid social comes back almost six times over, and that is the core of this raise.

## Use of funds

We will scale paid social from $150K to $400K per month over the next 12 months, since the returns above justify leaning in. At a $210 channel CAC, that buys roughly 1,900 new subscribers a month at full spend. We will also expand the influencer program and bring the Southeast co-packer online, opening shipping zones that today take three days.

- $7.0M paid acquisition
- $2.5M second co-packer onboarding and inventory
- $1.5M team (growth marketing, supply chain, customer care)
- $1.0M working capital and buffer

## Why now

Premium pet food is growing faster than the broader category, and owners increasingly treat dogs as family members whose diet deserves the same attention as their own. We have shown that we can acquire subscribers efficiently, keep them at predictable rates, and make money on every box. The Series A lets us turn a strong regional business into a national one.
